About the Firm

Shaping the Skyline Since 2004

Oscar Archi is an award-winning architectural firm dedicated to creating elegant, functional, and sustainable spaces that stand the test of time.

From intimate private residences to landmark commercial towers, our multidisciplinary team blends artistry with engineering precision. We believe architecture is more than buildings — it is the art of shaping how people live, work, and connect.

  • Innovative & Sustainable Design Solutions
  • Full-Service: Concept to Completion
  • BIM & 3D Visualization Expertise
  • Global Portfolio — Local Expertise
  • Award-Winning Interior Architecture
